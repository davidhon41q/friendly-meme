<h1><strong>Vietnam Travel Planning: A Complete Guide for First-Time Visitors</strong></h1>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam is an excellent destination for first-time international travelers who want to experience beautiful beaches, historic cities, scenic landscapes, local food, and affordable accommodation in one trip.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">From the lively streets of Ho Chi Minh City to the lantern-lit lanes of Hoi An and the limestone islands of Ha Long Bay, the country offers a mix of cultural experiences and outdoor adventures.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, planning a trip to Vietnam requires more than booking flights and choosing hotels. You need to consider the weather, visa requirements, transportation, accommodation locations, travel costs, and the distance between destinations.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A well-planned itinerary helps you experience more of the country without spending too much time in transit.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This guide covers everything you need to plan a Vietnam trip, including the best places to visit, when to travel, where to stay, how to get around, and common mistakes to avoid.</span></p>

<h2><strong>2. Choose the Best Time to Visit Vietnam</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam has different weather patterns across its northern, central, and southern regions. As a result, there is no single month that guarantees ideal weather throughout the country.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">According to the official</span><a href="https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/weather-and-climate-vietnam/"> <span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam Tourism weather and climate guide</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, March to May generally offers favorable conditions across much of the country, although individual destinations have their own seasonal patterns.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Northern Vietnam</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Northern Vietnam includes Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh, and Sapa.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">September to November: Often a pleasant period for exploring Hanoi and enjoying scenic landscapes.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">December to March: Cooler weather, mist, and occasional cold conditions, particularly in mountainous areas.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">April to June: Warmer temperatures and opportunities for sightseeing, although heat can increase.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">June to August: Hot, humid weather with a greater chance of heavy rain and storms.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Choose September to November if you want to explore Hanoi, take a Ha Long Bay cruise, or visit the rice terraces around Sapa. Pack warmer clothing if you plan to visit the northern mountains in winter.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Central Vietnam</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Central Vietnam includes Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue, and nearby coastal destinations.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">February to August: Generally a useful period to consider for beach holidays and outdoor sightseeing, depending on the destination and local conditions.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">September to December: Rain, storms, and flooding can affect parts of the region.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">March to May: Often suitable for combining city sightseeing, beaches, and cultural attractions.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hoi An and Da Nang are especially appealing for travelers who want a combination of beach time, restaurants, shopping, and cultural experiences.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Southern Vietnam</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Southern Vietnam includes Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta, and Phu Quoc.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">December to April: Generally drier conditions and a good period for city sightseeing.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">May to November: The rainy season brings heat, humidity, and frequent showers, often in the afternoon.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">July to September: Conditions around Phu Quoc can be wetter and stormier, which may affect beach activities and ferry services.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Rainy-season travel can still be enjoyable. Just allow flexibility in your schedule and check the forecast before booking boat trips or outdoor excursions.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Planning tip: Vietnam's tropical storm season can disrupt flights, road journeys, and cruises. Check current weather warnings before departure, particularly if your trip falls between May and November.</span></p>
<h2><strong>3. Select the Best Places to Visit in Vietnam</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam offers a wide range of experiences, from historic landmarks and mountain scenery to modern cities and tropical islands. Instead of trying to visit every attraction, select destinations that match your interests.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Hanoi: History, Street Food, and Local Culture</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 2 to 3 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hanoi is a strong starting point for a northern Vietnam itinerary. Its Old Quarter features narrow streets, traditional shops, street-food stalls, cafes, and markets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Popular places to explore include:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hoan Kiem Lake and the surrounding walking area</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Old Quarter and its traditional streets</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Temple of Literature</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum complex, subject to its opening schedule</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local markets, coffee shops, and Vietnamese restaurants</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Try local dishes such as pho, bun cha, and banh mi. If you enjoy food experiences, consider a guided street-food walk to learn about local ingredients and cooking traditions.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hanoi also works well as a base for day trips and overnight excursions to Ninh Binh and Ha Long Bay.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Ha Long Bay: Limestone Islands and Boat Cruises</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 1 to 2 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ha Long Bay is known for its limestone formations, emerald waters, and scenic boat cruises. It is one of the most recognizable landscapes in Vietnam.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can choose a day cruise if your schedule is tight or an overnight cruise if you want more time on the water.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Depending on the operator and itinerary, activities may include kayaking, visiting caves, exploring small islands, and enjoying sunset views from the boat.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Compare cruise inclusions carefully. Some packages include meals and transfers, while others charge extra for transportation or activities. Weather conditions can also affect departure times and itineraries.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Ninh Binh: Countryside, Rivers, and Limestone Mountains</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 1 to 2 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ninh Binh is a good choice if you prefer natural scenery over busy city streets. The region features rivers, limestone cliffs, temples, and green countryside.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Popular experiences include:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Taking a boat trip through Trang An or Tam Coc</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visiting the ancient capital area of Hoa Lu</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Climbing to the viewpoint at Hang Mua</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cycling through rural villages and rice fields</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ninh Binh is particularly suitable for couples, photographers, and travelers who want a slower pace after spending time in Hanoi.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Da Nang: Beaches and Convenient City Travel</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 2 to 3 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Da Nang combines city conveniences with easy access to the coast. It has long sandy beaches, seafood restaurants, modern hotels, and access to several popular attractions.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Consider visiting My Khe Beach, the Marble Mountains, and the Son Tra Peninsula. You can also explore the Dragon Bridge area, especially in the evening.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Da Nang is a practical base for travelers who want to combine beach time with day trips. However, check local weather conditions before planning swimming, boat trips, or other outdoor activities.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Hoi An: Lantern-Lit Streets and Traditional Architecture</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 1 to 2 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hoi An is famous for its historic architecture, riverside setting, lanterns, tailor shops, and traditional food. The Ancient Town is particularly appealing in the late afternoon and evening.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Things to do include:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Walking through the Ancient Town</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Exploring local markets and independent shops</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Trying cao lau, a regional noodle dish</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cycling through nearby villages</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visiting An Bang Beach when the weather is suitable</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hoi An is close enough to Da Nang for a day trip, but staying overnight allows you to enjoy the town after many daytime visitors have left.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Ho Chi Minh City: Modern Vietnam and Historic Landmarks</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 2 to 3 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ho Chi Minh City, formerly known as Saigon, offers a different atmosphere from Hanoi. It is energetic, modern, and filled with cafes, restaurants, shopping areas, museums, and nightlife.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Include these places in your itinerary:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The War Remnants Museum</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Independence Palace</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ben Thanh Market</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nguyen Hue Walking Street</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Central Post Office and nearby historic landmarks</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local cafes and street-food areas</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can also arrange a day trip to the Cu Chi Tunnels or the Mekong Delta, depending on your interests and available time.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Phu Quoc: Beaches and a Relaxed Island Holiday</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Recommended stay: 2 to 4 days</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Phu Quoc is a good option for travelers who want to finish their holiday with beaches, resort stays, seafood, and a slower pace.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Popular activities include swimming in suitable conditions, visiting beaches, taking boat excursions, and exploring local markets.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check the seasonal weather before booking. Rough seas and heavy rain can affect boat trips, ferry schedules, and beach conditions.</span></p>
<h2><strong>4. Plan Your Accommodation Before Booking Flights and Activities</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Your hotel location can significantly affect the quality of your trip. A low room rate may not be worthwhile if you spend extra money on taxis or lose time traveling to the attractions you want to visit.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Start by identifying the main activities in each destination. Then choose accommodation within convenient reach of those areas.</span></p>

<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Thao Dien in Thu Duc City: Popular with travelers seeking international restaurants, cafes, and a more residential atmosphere.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Before choosing a hotel, compare its exact location, transport options, recent guest reviews, cancellation terms, and included facilities.</span></p>

<h3><strong>Where to Stay in Hanoi</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hanoi's Old Quarter is a practical choice for first-time visitors who want restaurants, markets, and major central attractions within walking distance.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hoan Kiem is another convenient area, particularly for travelers who want to explore the lake and nearby historic sites. <h3><strong>Where to Stay in Da Nang and Hoi An</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Da Nang, choose a beachside hotel if you want easy access to the sea and coastal restaurants. Staying closer to the city center may suit travelers who prioritize local food, shopping, and transport connections.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In Hoi An, staying near the Ancient Town makes evening walks easier. Accommodation farther out may offer more space, gardens, or quieter surroundings, but you may need to arrange transport.</span></p>

<h2><strong>5. Understand Vietnam's Visa Requirements</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visa requirements depend on your nationality, passport, purpose of travel, and intended length of stay. Check the rules that apply to your passport before booking non-refundable flights.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For Indian passport holders, the Embassy of India in Hanoi advises travelers to obtain the appropriate visa before entering Vietnam. It also recommends checking that the visa details match the passport and travel plans.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam's official electronic visa portal states that e-visas can be issued for up to 90 days, with single or multiple entry depending on the visa granted. Approval is not automatic, and the permitted dates and entry conditions must match your trip.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use these steps when preparing your visa application:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visit the official</span><a href="https://evisa.gov.vn/"> <span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam e-Visa portal</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Read the eligibility and application instructions.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Enter your passport details and travel information accurately.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Select the applicable entry and exit points.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pay the required application fee through the official system.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check your application status and download the approved visa.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Verify your name, passport number, validity dates, and entry details before departure.</span></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep a digital copy of your visa and a printed copy with your travel documents. Do not rely on an application receipt as proof of visa approval.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Important: Visa rules and application procedures can change. Check the official portal and relevant embassy guidance shortly before your trip rather than relying solely on older blog posts or social media advice.</span></p>
<h2><strong>6. Book Flights and Plan Your Route</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam has international airports serving major destinations, including Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang. The best arrival airport depends on your itinerary and available flight connections.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you plan to explore northern Vietnam, arriving in Hanoi may reduce unnecessary travel. If your priority is southern Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh City is a logical starting point. Travelers focusing on central Vietnam can compare flights to Da Nang.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Before booking, compare:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Total journey time, including layovers</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Checked baggage allowance</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Arrival and departure times</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Airport transfer costs</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Change and cancellation policies</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The cost of domestic flights between destinations</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For a trip covering several regions, consider an open-jaw itinerary. This means flying into one city and departing from another, which can reduce backtracking.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For example, you could arrive in Hanoi, travel south through central Vietnam, and depart from Ho Chi Minh City. Compare the full cost with a return flight before deciding.</span></p>
<h2><strong>7. Choose the Best Way to Travel Around Vietnam</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam offers domestic flights, trains, buses, taxis, and app-based transport. The best option depends on the distance, your budget, and how much time you have.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Domestic Flights</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Domestic flights are useful when you need to cover long distances. A flight can save substantial time compared with an overland journey between northern and southern Vietnam.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, include airport transfers, check-in time, baggage rules, and possible delays when comparing total journey times.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Trains</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Train travel offers a slower way to see the countryside. It can work well for selected routes, particularly when you want a scenic journey or an overnight sleeper service.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Book ahead for popular dates, overnight cabins, and Vietnamese public holidays. Check the route and travel duration before assuming that the train will be faster or cheaper than flying.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Buses and Sleeper Buses</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Buses connect many cities and tourist destinations. They can be useful for budget travelers, especially on routes between places such as Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue, and other central destinations.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check the operator's reviews, luggage policy, departure location, and expected arrival time. An inexpensive ticket may be less attractive if the bus arrives far from your hotel or takes substantially longer than expected.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Taxis and Ride-Hailing Apps</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Taxis and ride-hailing services are practical for airport transfers and short journeys within cities. Check the fare estimate and destination before starting the ride.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At airports, use official pickup areas or follow the instructions in your transport app. Avoid accepting unsolicited offers from people claiming to provide rides.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Renting a Motorbike</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Motorbikes are common in Vietnam, but traffic can be challenging for inexperienced riders. Do not rent one simply because it looks like the easiest way to explore.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check local licensing requirements, insurance coverage, helmet quality, and rental conditions. If you lack suitable riding experience or documentation, choose a car, taxi, or guided excursion instead.</span></p>
<h2><strong>8. Try Vietnamese Food and Local Experiences</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Food is one of the highlights of traveling in Vietnam. You can find inexpensive street meals, traditional family-run restaurants, specialty coffee shops, and upscale dining.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Some dishes worth trying include:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pho: Noodle soup commonly served with beef or chicken.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Banh mi: A filled baguette with a variety of meat, vegetables, herbs, and sauces.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bun cha: Grilled pork served with noodles, herbs, and dipping sauce.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cao lau: A noodle dish associated with Hoi An.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Goi cuon: Fresh rice-paper rolls with fillings that vary by preparation.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnamese iced coffee: Coffee served over ice, often with condensed milk.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Food preferences and dietary requirements need some planning. If you are vegetarian, ask whether the broth, sauce, or cooking oil contains meat or seafood. For allergies, communicate the specific ingredient you need to avoid rather than relying on a general description.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beyond food, consider a cooking class, a traditional market visit, a countryside cycling tour, or a guided walk through a historic neighborhood. These experiences can help you understand local culture rather than simply visiting landmarks.</span></p>
<h2><strong>9. Prepare for Money, Internet, and Everyday Payments</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnamese dong (VND) is the local currency. Before traveling, check the current exchange rate and compare the fees charged by your bank, card provider, or currency exchange service.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep some cash available for small purchases, markets, local eateries, and places that do not accept cards. Use reputable exchange counters and check the amount before leaving the counter.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For internet access, compare an international roaming package, a travel eSIM, or a local SIM card. Confirm that your phone supports the option you choose and that you can complete any required registration.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A few practical habits can make everyday transactions easier:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check prices before ordering or booking.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Confirm the fare before using a transport service that does not provide a clear estimate.</span></li>

</ul>

<h2><strong>13. Common Vietnam Travel Planning Mistakes to Avoid</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Even a well-researched trip can become stressful when you overlook small but important details.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Trying to Visit Too Many Places</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vietnam's geography makes travel between distant regions time-consuming. A route that looks short on a map may require a flight or a long overland journey.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Choose a few destinations and allow enough time to explore them properly.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Ignoring Regional Weather Differences</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A beach destination may have excellent weather while another region experiences heavy rain. Check conditions for each stop rather than relying on a single nationwide forecast.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Booking Accommodation Based Only on Price</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A cheap room far from the places you want to visit may increase your transport expenses. Compare location, reviews, room conditions, and cancellation terms alongside the nightly rate.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Leaving Visa Applications Until the Last Minute</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visa processing times can vary. Apply through the official portal with enough time to resolve errors, and do not assume your application has been approved until you receive the result.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Underestimating Travel Time</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Include airport transfers, check-in time, station transfers, and road conditions when planning each day. Avoid scheduling a major tour immediately after a long overnight journey.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Forgetting to Check Inclusions</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A tour price may not cover meals, entrance tickets, transfers, or optional activities. Read the inclusions before paying and calculate the total cost.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Failing to Plan for Bad Weather</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Heavy rain and tropical storms can affect cruises, flights, ferries, and road journeys. Keep some flexibility in your itinerary and review cancellation policies before booking weather-sensitive activities.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use this checklist to organize your preparations.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check passport validity and applicable entry requirements.</span></li>

<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Arrange travel insurance.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Check weather forecasts and regional seasonal conditions.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Prepare suitable payment methods and some local currency.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Arrange mobile data or roaming.</span></li>
